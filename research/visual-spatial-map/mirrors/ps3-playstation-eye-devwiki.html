Video and Audio[edit | edit source]

Video[edit | edit source]

  • 640×480 pixels @ 60 Hz
  • 320×240 pixels @ 120 Hz

Audio[edit | edit source]

  • 16-bit audio @ 48 kHz
  • SNR (sound to noise ratio) @ 90 decibles
