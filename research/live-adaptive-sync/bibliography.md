# Bibliography: Live Adaptive Sync

## Sampling-Rate Offset / Clock Drift

- L. Wang and S. Doclo, "Correlation Maximization-Based Sampling Rate Offset Estimation for Distributed Microphone Arrays," IEEE/ACM TASLP, 2016.  
  Mirror: `mirrors/wang-doclo-2016-correlation-maximization-sro.pdf`

- J. Schmalenstroeer and R. Haeb-Umbach, "Sampling Rate Synchronisation in Acoustic Sensor Networks with a Pre-Trained Clock Skew Error Model," 2013.  
  Mirror: `mirrors/schmalenstroeer-haeb-umbach-2013-pretrained-clock-skew.pdf`

- P. Didier, T. van Waterschoot, S. Doclo, and M. Moonen, "Sampling Rate Offset Estimation and Compensation for Distributed Adaptive Node-Specific Signal Estimation in Wireless Acoustic Sensor Networks," 2023.  
  Mirror: `mirrors/didier-2023-sro-estimation-compensation-wasn.pdf`

- "On Synchronization of Wireless Acoustic Sensor Networks in the Presence of Time-varying Sampling Rate Offsets and Speaker Changes," arXiv:2110.12820.  
  Mirror: `mirrors/synchronization-time-varying-sro-speaker-changes.pdf`

- "Online Estimation of Sampling Rate Offsets," EUSIPCO 2021.  
  Mirror: `mirrors/online-estimation-sampling-rate-offsets-2021.pdf`
